What the file needs

One row per position, with a header row. Column names are detected automatically — ticker, symbol, quantity, shares, cost, avg price and common German equivalents all work — and you can correct the mapping after loading. 

ticker,quantity,cost_basis
AAPL,40,182.40
MSFT,15,395.10
XOM,60,104.25

Cost basis is per share and optional — without it you get valuation and risk, but no profit and loss.

API keys

Keys stay in this browser (localStorage) and are sent only to the service they belong to. Nothing is committed, logged or proxied. Every key is optional — the app tells you what each one unlocks and shows “—” for anything it cannot compute without one.
